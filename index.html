About

I am a platform engineer with twelve years of experience designing, scaling, and securing cloud-native infrastructure. I currently work in a Center of Excellence at Tata Consultancy Services, where I specialize in next-generation Internal Developer Platforms — bridging complex Kubernetes infrastructure and the workflows developers actually want to use.

My work combines system architecture with a strong emphasis on developer experience: custom Go controllers, practical RBAC, and paved roads that reduce cognitive load instead of adding another portal to learn. I previously spent several years on the OpenShift Observability team at Red Hat, and I contribute to CNCF projects including Meshery.

I studied computer science at the Indian Institute of Science in Bangalore, and I used to live in London.
